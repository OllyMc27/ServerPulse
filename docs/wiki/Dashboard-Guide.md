# Dashboard Guide

ServerPulse opens on a compact overview. Detailed records remain hidden until an administrator selects the question they want to investigate. Period selectors apply 24-hour, 7-day, 30-day or 90-day windows.

## Traffic & retention

Compare human traffic by server using online population, sessions, unique players, average completed-session length, returning-player rate and short-session rate. Low-sample servers are labelled as collecting data rather than being overinterpreted.

## Rotation performance

Map and mode pairs show rounds, population at the beginning and end, joins, leaves and survival. Filters separate reliable samples from low-sample rotations and highlight pairings that gain or lose players.

Use this view to test rotation changes rather than assuming the most-played map is automatically the best-retaining map.

## Busy times & exits

The heatmap aggregates human population across the network in the configured dashboard timezone. Disconnect outcomes use the best reason IW4MAdmin supplied; an ordinary quit and an unclassified lost connection may share the same fallback label.

## Player audience

Country rows show only privacy-safe samples. Flags are rendered locally from ISO country codes—there is no external flag service or tracking request. Popular time is shown in the dashboard timezone, not the player's local timezone.

## Community Voice

Read categorised, redacted excerpts with server, map, mode, time and anonymous-player context. Filters narrow the feed by topic and server. Signal rates are normalised per 100 completed player-hours to make periods with different traffic volumes easier to compare.

## Player Guidance

Review accusation signals, official report commands, reminder delivery, report follow-through, repeated targets, unique accusers and staff escalations. Resolved targets link to their IW4MAdmin profile.

## Action plan

Recommendations include a severity, confidence and sample size. They are prompts for measured experiments—not automatic configuration changes.

## Data health

Check configuration warnings, storage state, retained counts, current server telemetry and open monitoring incidents. A dash for RCON or event latency means the installed IW4MAdmin host does not expose that metric.
